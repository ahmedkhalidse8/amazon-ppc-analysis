# Amazon PPC Analysis – Key Insights

## 1. Overall Performance

- Total Sales: $7.27K
- Ad Spend: $5.2K
- ACOS: 94.67%
- ROAS: 1.06

### Insight:
The account is highly inefficient. ACOS is extremely high, meaning most revenue is being consumed by advertising costs.

---

## 2. Funnel Performance

- Impressions: 695K
- Clicks: ~6K
- Purchases: Very low compared to traffic

### Insight:
Strong top-of-funnel traffic but weak conversion efficiency. The issue is not visibility — it is conversion and targeting quality.

---

## 3. Campaign Performance

### Top Performing Campaign:
- BROAD MATCH CAMPAIGN
- Generated the highest sales volume

### Problem:
- Most campaigns have ACOS above 90%
- Some campaigns exceed 100% ACOS (losing money)

### Insight:
Campaigns are driving sales but not profit. Budget allocation is not optimized.

---

## 4. Keyword & Search Term Insights

### Best Performing Search Terms:
- "batana oil for hair growth"
- "hair growth oil"

These generate strong sales and should be scaled.

### Problematic Terms:
- Many search terms have spend but zero sales
- Several irrelevant or low-intent keywords are present

### Insight:
Account lacks proper keyword filtering and negative keyword strategy.

---

## 5. Major Problems Identified

- High ACOS across campaigns
- Poor keyword targeting
- No negative keyword control
- Budget wasted on non-converting terms
- Weak conversion efficiency despite high traffic

---

## 6. Recommendations

### Immediate Actions:
- Pause or reduce bids on high ACOS campaigns (>100%)
- Add negative keywords for non-converting terms
- Increase bids on top-performing keywords

### Optimization Strategy:
- Move converting search terms into exact match campaigns
- Reduce reliance on broad targeting
- Focus budget on high ROAS keywords

### Long-Term:
- Improve listing conversion (CTR + CVR)
- Test creatives and product positioning
- Continuously monitor wasted spend

---

## Final Conclusion

The account has strong traffic and demand signals, but profitability is severely impacted due to inefficient ad spend and lack of keyword control.

With proper optimization, this account has high potential for improved ROAS and profitability.
